The Origin Story

MEET

On February 8, 2026, a miracle happened at Rolling Hills Zoo in Salina, Kansas. A male southern white rhinoceros calf was born — and the internet fell in love.

Mom Evey (19) and dad Kengele (27) brought into the world a legend. Staff named him Creed — a nod to the KC Chiefs' own Creed Humphrey, who visited to meet his namesake.
